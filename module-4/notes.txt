class Student(name, cohort)
class Cohort(name, assigned_tm())
class Tm(name, cohort)

class Enrollment(student_id, cohort_id)

1 student has 1 cohort
1 cohort has many students

1 to many relationship between student and cohort

1 cohort has many TMs
1 TM has one cohort

1 to many relationship between cohort and TM

1 student has many TMs
1 TM has many students

A many to many relationship between Students and TMs
This relationship is imposed by the Enrollment class(intermediary/join class)
